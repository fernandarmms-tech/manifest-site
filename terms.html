Welcome

Who can use Manifest

You must be at least 13 years old, or older if the law where you live requires it. You are responsible for your account and for keeping your password safe.

Your content

You own what you post: videos, captions, comments, messages, photos and phrases. By posting it, you allow Manifest to store it and show it to the people you chose (everyone signed in, or only your group) for as long as it stays in the app.

Only post content you have the right to share. Do not post other people's work without permission, and do not record or post people who did not agree to it.

What is not allowed

Harassment, bullying, threats, or content that attacks people for who they are.

Sexual or sexually suggestive content, and any content involving minors in a harmful way.

Violence, self-harm encouragement, dangerous activities, or anything illegal.

Spam, scams, impersonation, or misleading others about who you are.

Sharing someone else's private information.

Selling or advertising medical treatments, or promising guaranteed results to others.

Safety tools and moderation

You can report any video, comment, message, post or person, and you can block anyone. A blocked person no longer sees your content and you no longer see theirs.

We review reports and may remove content or suspend or close accounts that break these rules, with or without notice.

Automated accounts

Some posts come from accounts run by Manifest itself, always marked "Official" or "AI" and "Automated account by Manifest". They share the app's own library of phrases and exercises. They are not real people.

Wellbeing, not medical advice

The phrases, exercises and practices in Manifest are for general wellbeing and reflection. They are not medical, psychological, financial or professional advice, and they do not guarantee any result. If you are struggling, please reach out to a qualified professional or to local emergency services.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile > Delete my account, or by following the steps on the account deletion page. We may close accounts that break these Terms.
