Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Summary

Manifest collects only what it needs to run the app. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

What we collect and why

Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our sign-in provider), to create your account and let you sign in.

Profile: your name, profile photo, goals and the setting for what you want to see. Your name and photo are visible to other members; your goals and settings are private.

What you create: videos (with caption, text, chosen music and audience), comments, likes, group messages, group memberships and communities. These are shown to the people you chose.

Private tools: your vision board (photos, phrases and links to images on the web), tasks, habits and exercise notes. Only you can see these.

Safety: the reports you send and the people you block, used to keep the community safe.

On your phone only: reminder settings and scheduled notifications. These never leave your device.

Camera, microphone and photos

The app asks for the camera, microphone and photo library only when you choose to record or pick a video or photo. Nothing is recorded or read without that action.

Where your data is stored

Your account, content and files are stored with Supabase, our database and storage provider, on servers in the United States. Videos and photos are kept in private storage and shown through links that expire.

Images you add to your board from the web stay on the website they came from; Manifest keeps only the link.

Who can see what

Videos open to everyone are visible to all signed-in members. Group videos, messages and members are visible only inside the group. Your board, tasks, habits, notes and goals are visible only to you.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, content, files and activity are deleted. Copies may remain in our provider's backups for a limited time before they are removed.

Your choices and rights

You can see and edit your profile, delete anything you posted, and delete your whole account in the app at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or for its deletion by writing to the contact below.

Children

Manifest is not intended for anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. When the changes matter, the app will tell you.

Questions or requests: manifestlifeadmin@gmail.com
