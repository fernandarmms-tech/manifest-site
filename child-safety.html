Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation

Manifest has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everything on Manifest: profiles, photos, videos, comments, group chats and private messages.

What is not allowed

Any content that sexualizes minors, including images, videos, drawings, text and links.

Grooming: contacting a minor to build trust for sexual purposes, asking a minor for images or to meet, or moving a conversation with a minor somewhere private for those purposes.

Sextortion, trafficking, or offering, asking for or sharing CSAM in any form.

Manifest is for people aged 13 and over. Anyone under 13 may not use it.

How to report

In the app: open "More" on any video, post, comment or profile, or press and hold any message, and choose Report. You can also block the person, so neither of you can see or message the other.

By email: write to manifestlifeadmin@gmail.com. Please include the name of the account and what you saw. Do not send us copies of abusive images.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency number first.

What we do

We review every report of child safety concerns as a priority. Content that breaks these standards is removed, and the accounts involved are closed.

We report apparent child sexual abuse material and exploitation to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the competent national and regional authorities where the people involved are, and we keep the information they need to investigate as the law requires.

We comply with all child safety laws that apply to Manifest.

Contact

Our child safety point of contact can be reached at manifestlifeadmin@gmail.com.
