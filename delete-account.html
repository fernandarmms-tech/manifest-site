Delete your Manifest account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open Manifest, go to the Profile tab, tap "Delete my account" and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Send an email to manifestlifeadmin@gmail.com from the email address of your Manifest account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

You can remove individual items yourself at any time, in the app: delete a video you posted (Delete under the video), a comment, a group or private message (press and hold it), a picture on your vision board, or a habit. Each item is deleted right away.

To have other data deleted without closing your account, email manifestlifeadmin@gmail.com from the email address of your Manifest account and tell us what to delete. We will do it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your sign-in, profile and photo; your videos, comments, likes, group and private messages; groups you created; your vision board, tasks, habits and notes; and the reports and blocks you made.

Copies may remain in our provider's backups for a limited time before they are removed. Nothing is kept for any other purpose.
